Effective collision strengths for the forbidden lines of Co II, from the VizieR catalogue VI/148.
Paper: Storey, P. J., Zeippen, C. J., Sochi, T. (2016), MNRAS, 456, 1974-1981,
doi:10.1093/mnras/stv2785, arXiv:1509.03164.

storetetal2016-co-ii.txt is the VizieR export of 2016-10-27 for the 15 lowest levels of Co II.

No code reads this file. It is a reference for a comparison with the Co II collision strengths of
another source.
